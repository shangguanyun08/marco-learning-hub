# Harry's multiplication difficulty

From Session 4 onward, including every future session, keep multiplication simple:

- Use whole-number times-table facts with factors from 1 to 10.
- Fraction multiplication may use only a unit fraction (1/2 or 1/4) times a whole number no greater than 10, giving a whole-number result.
- Do not use mixed-number, decimal, or multi-digit multiplication.
- Apply these limits to word problems, area, scaling, and worked explanations too. A fixed conversion such as 1 pound = 16 ounces may be given directly, without a larger product.
- Keep each session at 16 questions, two per skill, unless the user changes that requirement.
- Preserve earlier submitted questions and first-answer scores. Give replacement questions new IDs and retain the original questions and server keys for older records.

The course tests enforce the multiplication policy for active questions in Session 4 and all later sessions. Retired questions and Sessions 1–3 preserve their original content.
