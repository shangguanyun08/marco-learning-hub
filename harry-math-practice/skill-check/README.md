# Harry’s Daily Math · Eight skill checks

Five mixed sessions across eight skills. Sessions 1–2 have **24 questions (three per skill)**. Sessions 3–5 have **16 questions (two per skill)**. The 96 active questions include twelve per skill across the course. The question order changes between sessions, and representations and unknowns vary. This is a new course: Day 3–Set 8 and all older saved answers remain in their existing course and storage.

The eight skills are fraction operations, calculation accuracy, area/missing dimensions, proportional scaling, rounding/estimation, unit conversion, coordinates, and median. They come from the October 30 STAR recording and the earlier Daily Math first-answer records. Questions and explanations are authored practice, not official STAR items. All 134 active and archived answer keys are checked by independent arithmetic or diagram tests.

Sessions 4 and 5 include the original mixed-number fraction multiplication again, restored at the parent's request on November 5. Other multiplication uses whole-number times-table facts with factors 1–10, including area, scaling, and worked examples. Future sessions follow this policy, recorded in `AGENTS.md`, `multiplicationPolicy`, and the course tests. Original and replacement question versions retain their IDs and answer keys for saved records.

Harry may answer the questions in any order and change selections before pressing **Submit all 24 answers** or **Submit all 16 answers**, according to the set. Selections save on the current device and survive reloads. Submission is enabled only when every question has a choice; it saves all remaining first answers together and reveals the first-try score and right/wrong marks. Each mistake then gets one second try, saved on the current device without changing the first answer or score. Correct answers and explanations for mistakes appear after that second try. Earlier submitted first answers remain locked. Sessions open in order when the previous set is submitted. The parent can filter mistakes by skill and mark them reviewed after the retry. Optional working notes are saved but not graded.

The progress report uses completed sessions only. Its transparent checkpoint for considering another STAR test is: at least three sessions complete on at least two Pacific study dates; at least 8/9 accuracy in every skill across the most recent three finished sessions, rounded up to a whole-question target for the available count, including at least one success in every checked subskill; and at least 22/24 or 15/16 in each of the most recent two finished sessions. All earlier first-answer scores and mistake history are retained. The report calls this strong practice evidence, not a formal mastery diagnosis or a STAR score prediction. A follow-up review can identify which fresh checks are still needed.

Online storage is isolated under `harry-math-eight-skills-20261030-v1`. Local and remote answers merge without replacing the first answer accepted online. Conflicting offline choices are retained separately in the downloadable record. Server validation checks question IDs, answer keys, completion, and append-only first-answer preservation; stale writes require a version merge. The course also registers Harry’s math study time and completed sessions.
