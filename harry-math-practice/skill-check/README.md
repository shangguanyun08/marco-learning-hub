The eight skills are fraction operations, calculation accuracy, area/missing dimensions, proportional scaling, rounding/estimation, unit conversion, coordinates, and median. They come from the October 30 STAR recording and the earlier Daily Math first-answer records. Questions and explanations are authored practice, not official STAR items. All 120 answer keys are checked by independent arithmetic or diagram tests.

Harry may answer the 24 questions in any order and change selections before pressing **Submit all 24 answers**. Selections save on the current device and survive reloads. Submission is enabled only when every question has a choice; it saves all remaining first answers together and reveals the first-try score and right/wrong marks. Each mistake then gets one second try, saved on the current device without changing the first answer or score. Correct answers and explanations for mistakes appear after that second try. Earlier submitted first answers remain locked. Sessions open in order when the previous set is submitted. The parent can filter mistakes by skill and mark them reviewed after the retry. Optional working notes are saved but not graded.
