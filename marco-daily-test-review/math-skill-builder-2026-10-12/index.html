What this course checks

Rates/proportions; graphs and data; absolute value/signs; percentages; algebra with separate conditions. Ten short foundation checks cover decimal powers, angles, and nets.

Most questions combine familiar mathematics with a changed condition, unknown, or representation. These are original practice questions inspired by recorded skill gaps—not official ISEE items or an exhaustive syllabus.

Sets 1–4 build skills. Sets 5–6 are independent mixed checks. Set 7 automatically chooses fresh follow-ups for up to three missed concepts. Set 8 is an optional mixed transfer challenge. All sets are untimed; opening a page does not start a timer.

Automatic adaptation uses answer records. It cannot judge the reasoning in written notes. A finished green card means completed, not long-term mastery.
