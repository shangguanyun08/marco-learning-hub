Elephant · original and short models

Harry’s original

Model aiming for 6/8 · Start here

A little elephant played by the river. Some men found a golden flower nearby. They thought it was gold and attacked the elephants.

The little elephant ran away and lost his family. Bober stayed to help. They followed footprints and found the family.

The men returned. The little elephant sprayed them with water. Bober showed them that the flower was not gold, so they left.

Bober shared a rescue video. MrBeast, IShowSpeed, and Ronald came to hold a soccer game to help the elephants. Bober was the referee. The game ended 2–0, and the elephant was safe with his family.

Eli kicked a ball by the river. Nearby, some men found a golden dandelion. Thinking it was treasure, they threw a bomb to frighten the elephants away.

When the smoke cleared, Eli was alone.

“I’ll help you,” Bober whispered, holding out an empty hand. Eli stepped back, then gently touched his fingers.

They followed footprints until Eli heard his mother’s trumpet. He ran to her, and she wrapped her trunk around him.

The men returned with ropes. Eli trembled, but he stood before his family and sprayed them with water. Bober crushed a petal. “Flowers, not gold!” The men lowered their ropes.

Bober’s rescue video brought MrBeast, IShowSpeed, and Ronald to hold a soccer game to protect the elephants’ home. Eli scored. Bober blew his whistle. Two–zero!

This time, everyone was cheering. Nobody had to run.
