Elephant · original and model

Harry’s original

Model aiming for 8/8

While his family rested by the river, a little elephant named Eli kicked a stone between two trees. He trumpeted whenever he scored.

Nearby, some men found a golden dandelion. Thinking it was treasure, they threw a bomb to drive the elephants away. The herd fled through the smoke. When Eli stopped running, his mother was gone.

One man stayed behind. Bober loved elephants and hated what his companions had done.

“I’ll help you,” he whispered, holding out an empty hand.

Eli stepped back. Then he touched Bober’s fingers with his trunk.

They followed muddy footprints until a familiar trumpet echoed through the trees. Eli rushed to his mother. She wrapped her trunk around him.

Suddenly, the men returned with ropes. Eli trembled, but he stood in front of his family. He blasted the men with water, knocking them into the mud.

Bober rubbed a petal. “Look—yellow pollen, not gold!” The men stared at his dusty fingers and lowered their ropes.

Bober shared a video of the rescue. MrBeast, IShowSpeed, and Ronald came to organize a soccer game to raise money for the elephants’ home. Bober was the referee, and Eli scored the winning goal.

Two–zero!

This time, the noise was cheering. Nobody had to run.
