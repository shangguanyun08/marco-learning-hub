About these records

Difficulty 1–5 is an editorial estimate of word familiarity for this extension. Priority sources lead within each band, followed by higher recorded correct rates. This ordering combines word difficulty with Marco’s saved record.

EDUBUS identifies words appearing in the saved 59 spring and 20 summer Quizlet set inventories. Mock tests identifies recorded misses in Paper Mocks 0–3 or Zozeck 2026 mock synonym practice. Test Innovators identifies original misses from the four dated VR test reviews from September 27 through October 22. Source counts describe the chosen list before search and filters. A word can belong to multiple sources, while the priority total counts it once.

Extra review excludes words in the selected practice lists and words confidently known. Covered contains other review words already in those lists. The earlier 299-word core is included in the current daily list. Overlapping words are counted once. Section 16 repeats words from the current daily list.

Each correct rate is the number of correct saved checks divided by all saved checks, rounded to a whole percent. Wrong and unknown answers count in the total. The records include question choices, reviewed meanings and AI checks. History labels each kind. Accented spelling variants count as the same word.

A word moves into Known after correct checks on at least two different Pacific dates following its latest miss, including at least one AI-checked or reviewed short meaning. Same-day repetitions and self-reported “I know” choices do not meet this threshold. Uncertain ordering around undated misses is kept in Review. You can restore any Known word to Review.

The historical collection was compiled on November 9, 2026 from recovered VR questions, spring and summer vocabulary, mock reviews, unknown choices and checked short meanings. It includes words later answered correctly.

New archive checks and the existing Marco AI-test results load from online records. Other practice records reflect the collection date. “I don’t know” counts as a miss. A blank or ungraded meaning alone does not establish a wrong answer.

Copied answer records are counted once. Missing dates stay “Date not recorded.” For date-only records, same-day checks are kept in history without assuming which came first. Undated correct records appear in the total, with their order left unassigned.

Two malformed source items were excluded. Earlier correct question choices and reviewed short meanings are labelled separately from AI checks. Dates and times use Pacific Time. Typed answers are stored privately, while result history is public.
