How these questions were selected

All 80 VR questions in the supplied recording were checked. This set keeps 12 from each test: the harder vocabulary and the precision traps most relevant to Marco’s recent mistakes. Synonyms display just the target word; sentence wording and A–D choice order are retained, with blank lengths normalized. The meanings, tricks, and explanations are written for this session.

Difficulty is an editorial estimate—not an official ISEE or Test Innovators rating. Some book distractors are easier than the hardest Test Innovators questions. One easier transfer-check sentence is intentionally retained because it rechecks a word and skill Marco recently missed; its target is identified only after answering.

Focus areas: exact meanings and opposites, less-familiar vocabulary, whole-sentence clues, and contrast or restraint. Related mock mistakes appear only after a question is finished, so they do not give away the method.
