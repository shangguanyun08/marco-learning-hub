# Word Meaning Check

425 unique headwords, mixed across difficulty levels: eight sections of 50 and one of 25. `practice-data.js` derives stable mixed membership without editing the original bank, IDs, source references, or old section submissions. Only one section is rendered at a time.

The initial union had 468 headwords: all 168 focused Kindle/Premium synonyms; all 300 mixed Zozeck synonyms; all 99 Review 5 synonyms (already in the mixed source); 20 synonym miss records and 12 sentence-completion target records from the four saved mock reviews. After the user requested relevance screening, 43 lower-priority formal/rare/narrow words were deferred. `curation.mjs` and `data.excluded` retain the exact decision trail. No actual target in the four mock reviews was removed. Retained source memberships: 167 focused, 258 mixed, 80 Review 5; these overlap and must not be added. Duplicates are merged by headword, not by meaning. Only main targets are included, never incidental choice words.

The approximate-grade column was removed at the user’s request. The original editorial grade metadata remains in the frozen bank but is not shown. Difficulty tiers remain source metadata; they do not determine the displayed practice order. Every mixed section contains words from all three bands.

`history-data.js` stores only minimum documented encounter counts and source aggregates, never raw student answers. `build-history.mjs --emit-history-patch` performs GET-only live reads for ten existing practice apps, plus the captured original Zozeck selections, summer Days 1–15 reports, Paper Mocks 0–3 documented misses, and the four original mock reviews. First answers count once per question per run/round; within-question second chances are not extra encounters. Mere bank membership, choices, definition-only tasks, and this new checklist are excluded. Counts are static as of the visible checked timestamp. Paper correct answers, unknown repeats, unsaved local work, and older Quizlet reading cannot be claimed as a complete lifetime history. Missing evidence is shown as “No record,” not zero/never seen. Exact source word matching only; derivatives are not silently conflated.

Each section has three rounds. All its words shuffle deterministically in each round; the order is stable on reload and across devices. Finish the current round with a meaning or explicit “I don’t know” for every word to unlock the next. Later rounds start blank. Round drafts and immutable submitted copies are stored separately; revisions append copies. No automatic correctness judgment is made. “I don’t know” reveals a 1–3-word reference meaning from `short-meanings.js`; toggling it back preserves any typed draft. Positive/negative tone controls have been removed. Historical tone data is still retained in original records and exports.

Independent app namespace: `marco-vr-meaning-check-20261026-v1`. The shared record upgrades from version 1 to version 2, retaining `entries` and original-section `submissions`. New `rounds` and `roundSubmissions` use mixed membership, including Round 1. Original meanings are visible by word ID in Round 1; fresh round edits do not overwrite the original entries. The browser uses a separate `:rounds-v2` key and keeps the old key untouched, with a `:before-rounds` backup. Fields merge by timestamp and online saves use version checks. A blank browser performs no POST. The progress score counts recorded first-round answers, never correctness.

The footer provides Finish Round and Start Round 2/3 buttons. Completed rounds can be revisited, and all their saved copies are available for manual review. Copy exports the visible round; Download includes every draft, submitted round, original entry, and original submitted section. No emails or AI reviews are sent automatically.

`?demo=1` isolates browser QA and disables production progress/time writes. Tests mock the API in memory; never submit fake production answers. Opening an empty list performs GET only.

`build-data.mjs --patch` emits an apply_patch payload to reproduce `data.js`. Original bank membership/order/IDs remain frozen under this namespace. The mixed view is derived separately; do not change its membership or shuffle seed after people have answered.

Tests: `node --test marco-vr-practice/meaning-check/tests.mjs` (jsdom via the shared NODE_PATH).
