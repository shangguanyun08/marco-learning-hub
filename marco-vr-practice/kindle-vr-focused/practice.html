What was kept—and why

73 of the earlier 146 questions retained + 172 selected from the PDF = 245. Five pages contain 40 questions each; the last contains 45. The 73 lower-priority earlier items remain in Previous Practice. They have not been marked mastered.

Selection considers the whole question: precise meaning, secondary meanings, part of speech, plausible choices, and sentence relationships. Common-looking words can stay when their tested use is tricky. Straightforward literal matches, obvious distractors, duplicate source questions, and off-target technical vocabulary were trimmed. Difficulty is an editorial estimate—not an official ISEE rating or a measured prediction of Marco’s score.

The supplied Premium PDF begins at Test 9. All 1,320 VR questions in Tests 9–41 were reviewed; 121 synonyms and 51 sentence completions were selected. Test 8 is retained from the earlier recording. Original stems and A–D choice order are preserved, with typographic cleanup. PDF answers were checked against the printed keys; their explanations are included after answering. Short meanings and tricks are written for this practice.

No source question repeats in this course. A word may recur in a genuinely different question or context. Predictions and elimination are optional. A wrong first answer gets a second try before the answer, meaning, and trick appear; a correct answer reveals them immediately.
