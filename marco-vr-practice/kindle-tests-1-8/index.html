Selection and source notes

24 existing selections from Tests 1–2 plus 122 new selections from Tests 3–8. Selected from eight 40-question VR tests, rather than retaining every routine item. Only completely readable questions and choices are included; partially visible items are excluded. Difficulty is an editorial judgment, not an official ISEE rating or a claim that Marco will find every item difficult.

Original question wording and A–D choice order are retained, with blank lengths and typographic punctuation normalized. Synonym prompts show only the main word. The new recordings do not include publisher answer keys: new answers were independently checked against the word meanings and sentence clues. Meanings, tricks, and explanations are written for this practice, not quoted publisher explanations.

Pages mix source tests without repeating the same source question. Some words recur in different original questions or contexts on purpose. Predictions and elimination are optional. A wrong first answer gets a second try before the answer, meaning, and trick appear; a correct answer reveals them immediately.
