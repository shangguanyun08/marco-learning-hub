1. Build word meaning in context

Revisit apprentice, gruesome, detect, cull, deteriorate, fundamental, articles, and exploit. For each word: reread the whole sentence, explain the word in simple language, identify the clue, and use it in a different sentence. Q30 and Q31 particularly need choosing the meaning that fits this context, rather than another familiar meaning.

2. Point to the exact evidence

For Q27, notice both cars are shiny red. For Q28, distinguish the body from the hairs on the spine. For Q33, identify what happens before the molds are dipped. Have Harry finish: “I chose this because the passage says …” before checking the answer.

3. Connect description, perspective, and realism

Q13 describes Sam’s appearance, not an eerie setting. Q21 asks specifically for Mei’s thoughts and feelings. Q16 requires connecting “three generations” to the cat’s implausible age. Practice naming whose perspective is being described, what a detail helps the reader understand, and whether a story event could realistically happen.

Strengths to keep using

Harry correctly identified the atmosphere passage’s main idea (Q11), summarized Lok’s decision (Q18), and found the story’s theme (Q23). He also drew supported conclusions about Gina and Clarence and predicted Minh’s next action (Q17, Q32, Q25). Ask him to use that same evidence-based thinking with individual words and details.

A manageable practice plan

  1. Keep enjoyable daily reading. Add a short discussion about one word and one passage detail; do not turn every reading session into a test.
  2. Review 3–4 missed questions at a time. Ask for his reasoning first, then compare the explanation. Avoid learning only the option number.
  3. Check transfer with fresh text. After a day or two, use new sentences for those words and a short new passage with a detail, sequence, or point-of-view question.
  4. Track independent explanations. Count a skill as improving when he can answer a new question and point to evidence without help, not only repeat a memorized answer.
